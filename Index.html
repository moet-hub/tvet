Fulbright University Viet Nam

fulbright.edu.vn

Brief Introduction

Founded in 2016, Fulbright University Viet Nam (FUV) is a not-for-profit foreign-invested university conducting research and training based on rigorous international standards. FUV's School of Public Policy and Management is the first in Southeast Asia to receive NASPAA accreditation. Fulbright serves the next generation of Vietnamese entrepreneurs, scientists, engineers, policy innovators, and artists through exceptional liberal arts and graduate programs that provide students with the skills and global adaptability to thrive in a fast-changing world.

Brief Introduction

As a premier multidisciplinary research hub, Vietnam National University is mandated by Decision 1600/QD-TTg to lead international academic integration. Ranked consistently among the top global institutions, VNU is aggressively expanding its joint training curricula and prioritizing strategic partnerships with top 500 global universities. Our extensive research portfolio is designed to tackle immediate regional challenges through collaborative innovation in engineering, natural sciences, and digital transformation.

Vietnam VET Center of Excellence

vet-coe.edu.vn

Brief Introduction

As a vanguard institution in Vietnam's vocational reform strategy under the Aus4Skills Phase 3 initiative, this Center of Excellence is dedicated to aligning local competencies with international industry demands. Utilizing the Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF), we specialize in rapid upskilling and the "9+ model" implementation. Our immediate goal is to establish secure educational pathways and facilitate structured labor mobility for Oceanic workforce deficits, particularly in high-demand technical and service sectors.
