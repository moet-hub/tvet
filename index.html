College of Machinery and Irrigation (VCMI)

Overview

The College of Machinery and Irrigation (VCMI) is a vocational education institution under the Ministry of Agriculture and Environment. Established in 1976, the college's main campus is located in Đồng Nai City, Vietnam. VCMI is one of the first 10 high-quality colleges in Vietnam, officially recognized under Decision No. 532/QĐ-BGDĐT issued by Vietnam's Ministry of Education and Training. The college currently has 266 staff, officials, and employees, and trains more than 6,000 students annually at the college and intermediate levels. VCMI currently offers training in a total of 16 occupations, 8 of which are delivered to international standards (6 occupations in Mechanical Engineering and Electrical–Electronics following German standards, and 2 occupations in Logistics following Australian standards). VCMI's development strategy is to become a high-quality green vocational training center, pursuing sustainable development and building a strong reputation within Vietnam's education system, the ASEAN region, and internationally.

Strategic Focus
Green Transformation Logistics (Aus Std) Mech & Auto Tech
